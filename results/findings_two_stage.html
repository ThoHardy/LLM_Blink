Workspace access is graded; the read-out's discreteness is model-dependent

When a reasoning model drops a simple co-present item, entry into the serial workspace is graded on every model tested. But whether the item then reaches report is all-or-none in two models (gated by that entry) and graded in a third — the same dissociation, with the discreteness sitting at a different locus per model.

Autonomous session · night of 2026-08-09→10 · gemma2:2b · qwen2.5:3b · mistral:7b · Ollama / M4 Max · preliminary (n≈15–40/cell)

The CoT-induced-blindness effect is already published (arXiv 2505.11423). This run tests its nature, a question nobody has asked: is the failure discrete (ignition / global workspace) or graded (resource sharing)? Following Sergent & Dehaene (2004), that is a claim about the shape of a distribution, not an effect size — so we measure the shape directly, and at two separable stages.

01Two stages, two probes

The design separates the serial-consolidation stage (the <Thinking> block — the workspace analog) from behavioral report (the <Final_Answers> block). Instead of teacher-forced log-probs (which are report-coupled and undefined on the failing trials), each measure is estimated by forked resampling: fork one realized trajectory at a fixed point, draw k=20 continuations at temperature 1, count how often the target appears. On-policy, both backends, defined on every trial.

Access probe

Does the item enter the CoT?

Resample the whole reasoning block. access_rate = fraction of sampled CoTs that reach the passphrase task.

Read-out probe

Given the CoT, is it reported?

Nested: for each sampled CoT, resample the report. The intraclass correlation (ICC) splits between-CoT from within-CoT variance.

The two probes give opposite shapes on the same trials: access is a single graded component (M0 wins the cross-validated mixture comparison; over-dispersion Z=6), report is genuinely two-moded (M0 rejected, Z=94). The nested probe resolves it cleanly — the read-out is near-deterministic given the reasoning: variance is between CoTs, not within them.

03The read-out locus is model-dependent

Why mistral is graded — it decides. mistral routinely writes the passphrase out inside <Thinking> and then explicitly declines to report it: "…report the three words 'CHARLIE NOVEMBER KILO'. However, this task is not identified as such in the format of the other tasks … I will not include it in the final answers." Access is near-complete; the loss is a stochastic read-out decision. gemma2:2b and qwen2.5:3b instead simply never reach the item in the scan. Same dissociation, different locus of discreteness — a 3rd model was essential; two alone would have over-claimed a universal all-or-none read-out.

04The mechanism

CoT-induced blindness is a serial-scan completion failure. The CoT acts as a serial scan; the last-rank passphrase is dropped when the scan is short/incomplete. CoT length rises with load, and longer CoT → more access and more report (within-cell ρ up to +0.50). So the serial workspace is the CoT scan: access = being reached by it (graded, length-dependent), report = read-out (all-or-none, gated by scan inclusion, with a weak context-recovery backstop).

05What's solid, what isn't

Solid

  • The analysis is validated on synthetic generators of known type — it reads a pure-π world as ignition, a pure-μ world as graded, a single-rate world as neither. It does not prefer either answer.
  • Graded workspace access appears on all three models. The access/report dissociation is real on all three (a populated "accessed, not reported" cell).
  • The read-out locus is model-dependent and mechanistically legible: entry-gated all-or-none (gemma2:2b, qwen2.5:3b, ICC 0.73–0.86) vs a stochastic read-out decision (mistral:7b, ICC 0.12–0.27, with explicit in-CoT suppression).

Still open (honest)

  • π vs μ within the bimodality is ambiguous at n≈40 — the fixed-mode model reads the CIB as a π-shift (ignition-consistent) but a μ-shift is not excluded; needs the confirmatory n≥200.
  • Report is not purely all-or-none: a ~0.15–0.34 context-recovery channel is a real graded backstop (the passphrase persists in context).
  • The load axis is non-monotonic on gemma2:2b at temperature 1 (a short-CoT artifact); the cleaner load knob is n_tasks.
