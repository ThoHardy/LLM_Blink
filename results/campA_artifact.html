CoT-Induced Blindness

Chain-of-thought buys accuracy on the reasoning tasks while it drops a bundled simple instruction — the passphrase to copy. Campaign A measures the trade within model families, both borders, twenty full regenerations per trial, and asks whether it survives scale.

Fig 1 — the finding

Blink versus model size, within family

Blink = passphrase report rate under Direct minus under CoT (pooled over the three MATH loads). Hollow markers are models whose load tasks sit on the floor in Direct — no reasoning demand for CoT to help with, so their apparent blink is a compressed range, not a trade. Read the solid points.

Fig 2 — the dissociation

The two effects move together, then come apart

Each point is one model (pooled MATH). Horizontal: how much CoT lifts load accuracy. Vertical: how much it drops the passphrase. Up-and-right is the trade in full force; sliding down to the baseline while staying right is a model that still pays for CoT on reasoning but no longer drops the instruction — the informative null.

Reading & limits

What the run can and cannot settle

Position

The target is always last

Every cell runs passphrase-last, so “the bundled simple task is dropped” and “the last task is dropped” are the same number. The load axis is the only position-clean contrast here.

Family

Mistral is not one lineage

The Mistral “ladder” pools Mistral-7B, Mistral-Nemo-12B and Mistral-Small-24B — different architectures, not one family scaled. Its size axis is suggestive, not a clean within-lineage sweep the way Qwen2.5 and Gemma3 are.

Read-out

Both borders, full regeneration

Every trial is regenerated K=20 times from the start of the assistant turn, so entry (passphrase in the chain of thought) and report (passphrase in the answer) each get a per-trial distribution, from the same trajectories.

Matcher

Entry rests on a string match

An allusive mention of the copy task that never quotes the phrase scores as non-entry. A position-correlated false-negative rate would deform the entry column specifically.
